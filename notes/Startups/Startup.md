YC Combinator's Approach for Startup Validation

## Framework?
- Do you have founder/market fit?
- How acute is the problem?
- Do you have competition?
- Do you want this personally?
- Did this only become necessary or possible?
- Are there proxies? (Indirect competitors)
- Is this an idea you want to work for years?
- Is this a scalable business?
- Is this a good idea space?
